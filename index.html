How a complaint moves through Softnear CRM

From first contact to closed, in four steps.

STEP 1

Log

Customer care records the complaint from phone, email, website, social or a store letter.

STEP 2

Investigate

Technical checks GMP records, taste panels and traceability, then writes the RCA.

STEP 3

Respond

AI drafts the letter or email. Your team edits, approves and records goodwill sent.

STEP 4

Report

Dashboards update instantly so trends and CPMU breaches are spotted early.

AI response generator

  • Only uses facts recorded in the investigation
